A note from the artist

Every event deserves a first impression

To me, a key visual is more than an image—it is the first impression of an event.

I enjoy taking a simple concept and turning it into a visual world through color, composition, typography, and imagery. Every project gives me an opportunity to experiment with different styles and discover new ways to communicate an idea.

I hope my designs can do more than catch the eye, but also create a feeling that stays with the viewer.

— Lian

★ Act I · Freshman Welcome Event ★

Act I · Key visual · Welcome Party

Black Hole Wonderland

The key visual for our department’s freshman welcome event. A girl in a space suit tumbles into a swirling black hole — a playful way of saying “welcome to a whole new universe.”

The lettering glows like starlight, with little sparkles tucked into the strokes.

★ Act II · Halloween Night Party ★

Act II · Key visual · Halloween

Bet On Her, She’s Trouble

The key visual for our Halloween party, themed as a spooky casino night: witches, card players, and pumpkins gather around the table under a heart-shaped spotlight.

Besides the poster and post, I designed the custom lettering and the event ticket — tucked right next to the poster.

★ Act III · Christmas Market ★

Act III · Key visual · Christmas Market

The Christmas Craft Fair

The key visual for our Christmas market — friends wander through a glowing fair of striped tents, gingerbread, and a giant Christmas tree.

The warm, rounded lettering was drawn to feel like the festival lights.
