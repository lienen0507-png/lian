A note from the artist

To the little girl who never stopped drawing

I have loved drawing since I was little, and I have always tried my best to improve. I’m grateful to my younger self for never giving up on practicing and for bringing me to where I am today.

Sometimes, I still wonder if I’m good enough or if anyone notices how hard I try. But I think my younger self would be happy to see who I am today.

The girl who once could only draw little stick-figure girls can now create an entire artwork on her own. I think she would be the person who is most proud of me.

And I’ll keep creating, learning, and moving forward with her.

— Lian

★ Tonight's Main Act ★

Main Act · No. 01 · Fairytale Reimagined

After starting university, I began challenging myself to create more polished artwork. As someone who loves cartoons and animation, I chose Snow White as a starting point, reimagining the character in my own style while exploring custom typography and visual design.

This project became an important starting point for me. Later, when I joined my department’s student association and began creating key visuals, I realized that this early experience gave me the confidence to take on new challenges.
