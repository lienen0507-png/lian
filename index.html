My Story

Hi, I’m Lian, a Digital Media Design student at National Pingtung University.

As a member of the Visual Design team in my department’s student association, I create event visuals, social media content, and promotional graphics.

I’m passionate about illustration, character design, and storytelling, and I’m always exploring new ways to bring ideas to life through animation, 3D, games, and visual design.
