The Pitch

What if a story could change reality?

The Reader is a story-driven pixel RPG where books don’t just record stories — they can change, or even destroy, reality.

The stories inside books are bound to the real world. When a story is altered, reality shifts along with it. Talk to the people you meet, fight when you must, and shape how the story ends — different ways of playing lead to different endings.

★ Once Upon a Page ★

The Story

Prologue

The World of Books

Where it all begins

❦

— 1 —

In this world, books are more than containers for stories. Every book holds a world of its own — and its characters, events, and endings all follow their own rules.

But when a story drifts from its path, the world inside begins to crumble, and the damage can spill into reality.

Those who repair these broken books, keeping story and reality in balance, are called Book Envoys. Isolde is one of them.

Calm and quiet, she prefers to work alone. Through conversations with other characters, players slowly uncover her hidden past — and the thoughts she keeps to herself.

— 2 —

Chapter 01

Snow White

A fairy tale gone wrong

❦

— 3 —

Isolde’s first mission: a copy of Snow White that has gone wrong. The story has lost its order, and players must explore the book, talk with its characters, and find the key object that sets it right — here, the poisoned apple.

There are two ways to repair a story:

Mercy Talk, build bonds, and gently guide the characters back to their roles.

Cruelty Ignore their feelings and force events to happen, pushing the story back on track.

Either way the book is mended — but every choice leads toward a different ending.

— 4 —

Chapter 02

The Apprentice

A new companion

❦

— 5 —

Back in the real world, Isolde meets her new apprentice: a 16-year-old girl who is her complete opposite — lively, curious, always talking, and often puzzled by Isolde’s cool way of handling things.

Together they take on new missions. From clashes and awkward first steps to understanding and trust, Isolde’s closed-off heart slowly begins to change.

Through their time together, players piece together fragments of Isolde’s past.

— 6 —

Chapter 03

The Unknown Story

A book no one has read

❦

— 7 —

One day, Isolde receives an unusual mission — not a familiar fairy tale, but an original story she has never seen before. With no clear clues, players must explore, talk, and follow the plot to find the true key object.

As the story unfolds, Isolde grows close to the book’s hero, and starts to look forward to every visit.

But between the book and reality, she keeps meeting mysterious menders in suits. They seem to know who she is — and keep trying to interfere. Slowly, Isolde senses she may be more than a mender of stories.

— 8 —

Final Chapter

The Truth Behind the Story

The page she was written on

❦

— 9 —

As the truth comes to light, Isolde finally realizes: she is not a Book Envoy in the real world. She is a character in a book.

The reality she has lived in, and every mission she has taken, are part of a story. The original book she could never understand is her apprentice’s autobiography — everything between them was already written.

And the menders in suits? They noticed something wrong with the story, and came to repair her.

Realizing her existence is bound by the story, Isolde begins to fight against her fate.

— 10 —

The Final Choice

Two Endings

Mercy or Cruelty

❦

— 11 —

Mercy If players often chose mercy, Isolde comes to understand the bond between characters, stories, and fate. Instead of only trying to escape her story, she learns to accept who she is — and searches for another possibility.

Cruelty If players always chose cruelty, Isolde is consumed by selfishness and fear. Obsessed with stopping the autobiography’s ending, she discovers its key object is her apprentice — and takes her life to destroy the story’s core. Yet everything she does was already part of the story.

— 12 —
