A note from the artist

Building worlds you can walk around

After entering university, I began exploring 3D modeling through various courses. Starting with simple models, I have gradually developed the skills to create complete small-scale scenes. This learning journey has required considerable time, effort, and dedication.

Beyond classroom instruction, I actively seek out online tutorials and learning resources to explore different modeling techniques and continuously improve my skills. The following works showcase the 3D models I created during my freshman year, reflecting my progress from learning the basics to building more complete scenes.

Now in my sophomore year, I have begun exploring character rigging, taking another step toward combining 3D modeling with character animation and expanding my skills in the 3D field.

— Lian

Timeline 01 / 05

★ Act I · Class Project ★

The Sweet Corner

A little café corner full of desserts — cupcakes, cakes, gingham tablecloths, and shelves of tiny treats.

Made for my Intro to 3D Graphics class, this was where I practiced modeling small objects, arranging a whole room, and lighting it warmly.

  • Interior scene
  • Food props
  • Composition
  • Made in Maya

Timeline 02 / 05

★ Act II · Diner Scene ★

Perfect Burger Meal

A retro American diner glowing with neon — checkered walls, red booths, chrome stools, and a perfect burger waiting on the counter.

I focused on colored lighting, reflective materials, and filling the room with small props that tell the story of a late-night meal.

  • Interior scene
  • Neon lighting
  • Props
  • Made in Maya

Timeline 03 / 05

★ Act III · Story Scene ★

Christmas Thief

Three little gnomes follow a scribbled treasure map into a late-night arcade, where burger coins spill across the checkered floor.

Told shot by shot, the story follows them as they sneak, play, and finally hide their loot under the Christmas tree — my first 3D piece built around a story.

  • Storytelling
  • Character Modeling
  • Environment Design
  • Made in Maya

Timeline 04 / 05

★ Act IV · Stylized Diorama ★

Cyran the Hedgefolk

Cyran, a little hedgefolk in a clock-badge hat, lives on a floating island with his cottage, workshop, and a barrel full of shiny gems.

I designed him in 2D first — front, side, and back views — then brought him and his world into 3D with soft, stylized materials and warm light.

  • Character Design
  • Modeling
  • Environment Design
  • Made in Maya

Timeline 05 / 05

★ Act V · 3D Self-Portrait ★

ME!

A 3D version of myself, standing on a little floating island of grass, berries, and sparkles.

I designed her in 2D first — a full-body drawing plus front, side, and back views — then modeled her from head to toe and built a dreamy stage around her.

  • Character Design
  • Character Modeling
  • Costume Design
  • Made in Maya
